\begin{center}
\underline{\textbf{Teorema fundamentală de izomorfism}}
\end{center}

\noindent\underline{Def}: Dacă $f:G\to G'$ morfism de grupuri, atunci

\[
\text{Im}\,f = f(G) \leq G'.
\]

\bigskip
\noindent\fbox{T} \ Dacă $f:G\to G'$ morfism $\Rightarrow \text{Im}\,f \cong \dfrac{G}{\ker f}$.

\bigskip
\noindent\underline{Dem}:

\[
\begin{tikzcd}
G \arrow[r,"f"] \arrow[d,"\pi"'] & \text{Im}\,f \subseteq G' \\
\dfrac{G}{\ker f} \arrow[ur, dashed,"f^*"'] &
\end{tikzcd}
\]

\bigskip
\noindent $\text{Im}\,f=f(G) \Rightarrow f:G\to\text{Im}\,f$ e surj.\ $\Rightarrow f^*=$surj.
